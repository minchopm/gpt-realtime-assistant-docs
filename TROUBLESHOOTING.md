# GPT Realtime Assistant — troubleshooting

Change one thing at a time and repeat a short test. Record the app version and mode before changing settings so the result can be reproduced.

## Transcript is inaccurate

Reduce competing speech, move the microphone closer and check the input language. Test names and specialist terms separately.

## Translation goes the wrong way

Check both selected languages and the two-way setting. Let one speaker finish before the other begins.

## No spoken translation

Check Speak Translations, device output volume and the current audio route. Distinguish written local answers from online spoken interpretation.

## Session cannot start online

Check connectivity, subscription status and available credits. Verify the app's displayed error rather than repeatedly creating sessions.

## Offline mode is unavailable

Confirm Apple Silicon Mac support, downloaded models and subscription requirements. Test before depending on it for a private session.

## A task or summary is wrong

Compare it with the source transcript and correct the owner, due date and decision status. AI summaries are drafts, not authoritative meeting records.

## Escalate with a small example

If the issue persists, include a sanitized reproduction and the observed result in a support request. Do not infer the cause from a single failed test or switch to a different processing mode without checking its data handling.
