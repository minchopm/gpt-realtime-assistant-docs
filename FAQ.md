# GPT Realtime Assistant — frequently asked questions

## Is this an official OpenAI app?

No. GPT Realtime Assistant is independently published by Arte Soft EOOD. Underlying provider names identify services, not endorsement.

## Do I bring my own API key?

The product site describes managed sessions with subscription credits and no user API-key setup.

## Can iPhone or iPad use the documented offline Mac mode?

The documented Whisper/Qwen workflow is specifically for Apple Silicon Mac. Do not infer the same local runtime on iPhone or iPad.

## Does local history mean online audio stays on-device?

No. Online voice sessions send audio to OpenAI. Saved transcripts and workspace items being device-local is a separate property.

## How long will my credits last?

There is no fixed allowance in minutes. Audio, text, model rates and session behavior affect usage. Check the balance and end sessions when finished.

## Can I use local mode without a subscription?

The current site says an active subscription is required even for zero-credit local Mac operation.

## Does it join every calendar meeting automatically?

The documented flow is user-started and user-ended; it is not described as an automatic meeting bot.

## Where should I start?

Use the quick-start guide with a small example, verify the saved result, then choose a practical workflow. Avoid beginning with irreplaceable data or an entire production project.

## How do I report a documentation or app problem?

Use the official product support channel for account or purchase issues. A public GitHub issue is suitable for documentation corrections and sanitized reproduction steps. Include the app version, device/OS, expected outcome and actual result; do not attach private data.
